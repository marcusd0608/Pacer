# Pricing (one page)

Principle: charge for the teacher's **time saved**, not for student seats, until a school has seen real usage.

| Plan | Price | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Trying it in class | Live rooms, QR join, anonymous confusion signals, check-in poll, board archive, a few AI answers per day |
| Teacher | $9/month or $79/year | Individual instructors, tutors, trainers | Unlimited AI help and post-class packages, saved lectures, exports, class rosters |
| School / department | About $50 to $100 per teacher per year, or $2 to $3 per student per year once usage is proven | Departments, charter networks, private schools | Admin overview, school sign-in, a signed data privacy agreement, support, onboarding |

## Why this shape
- **Per teacher first.** A teacher or department can approve it without a district purchasing process. Per-student pricing comes later, when a district trusts you.
- **AI is the cost.** Every AI answer costs real money. Free is capped per device; paid removes the cap. Keep gross margin at 80% or better by using a cheaper model for light tasks and caching repeats.
- **Annual discount** because school and tutoring budgets are annual and it reduces churn.

## Rough revenue math (so expectations are honest)
- 100 paying teachers at $79 is about $8k a year.
- $100k a year needs about 1,300 paying teachers, or about 7 mid-size school/department deals.

## Price tests to run in the first 90 days
1. Ask five teachers to pay $9 a month. Record yes, no, and the exact reason.
2. Ask two tutoring centers for $30 to $50 a month per site.
3. Never discount below $5 a month: if they only say yes at free, the problem isn't painful enough.
